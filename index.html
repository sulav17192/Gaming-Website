About

Brawl Stars is a third-person, top-down shooter game in which you play as your chosen Brawler and compete in a variety of Events. In these Events, you face off against other Brawlers as you attempt to complete a special objective unique to each type of Event.Brawl Stars takes place in a fictional abandoned amusement park named Starr Park. Initially introduced in a live-action short film,[7] in-game surveillance footage showed that Starr Park closed in 1995 due to magic crystals that granted several staff, visitors, and inanimate objects in Starr Park immortality, but also madness.[8]
